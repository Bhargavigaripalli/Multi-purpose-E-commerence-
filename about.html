Who We Are

Innovating the Future of Hyper-Commerce

Uniting next-generation electronics, farm-fresh groceries, designer fashion, and home living into a seamless 15-minute shopping ecosystem.

Our Story

Born to Unify Everyday Shopping

Founded in 2020, Stackly began with a bold vision: why should consumers jump between ten different apps for their laptops, organic groceries, urban clothing, and kitchen appliances? We built a centralized hypermarket platform that guarantees 100% brand authenticity, cold-chain freshness, and lightning-fast delivery under one roof.

Today, Stackly Multiverse powers over 2.5 million shoppers across India. Supported by 50+ robotic dark stores and nationwide cold-chain logistics, we deliver everything from crisp Washington apples and cold-pressed oils to flagship M3 MacBooks and designer streetwear within minutes.

👁️

Our Vision

To build the world's most intuitive multi-purpose commerce ecosystem, where any product—from fresh farm nutrition to cutting-edge artificial intelligence hardware—is accessible to everyone within minutes.

🎯

Our Mission

To eliminate retail compromises through verified direct-from-brand sourcing, cold-chain fresh guarantees, try-at-home fashion fitting, and 24/7 dedicated customer concierge care.

Chronology

Our Brand Journey

How we grew from a small local startup into India's leading online electronics hub

2020
The Foundation

Stackly began in Bangalore as a premier boutique hub for pro-grade computers, developer electronics, and custom workstations.

2022
